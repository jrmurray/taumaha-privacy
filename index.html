Taumaha Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Taumaha is a strength training and recovery tracker for iPhone with an optional Apple Watch companion. The app has no Taumaha account, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated server that receives training data.

Data on your devices

Taumaha stores your optional profile name, preferences, workouts, sets, notes, routines, exercise records, recovery sessions and Health Check answers on your iPhone. The app uses Health Check answers to apply fixed safety rules, such as pausing progression or shortening recovery limits. You can review or edit those answers in Profile. An Apple Watch paired to your iPhone receives the active workout and timer information needed to show and control the session. Widgets and Live Activities use on-device summaries or timer state.

iCloud backup

If iCloud is available, Taumaha can sync structured workout, routine and recovery records and eligible preferences in your Apple account’s private CloudKit storage. Taumaha keeps Health Check answers, safety flags, notes, previous routine versions and detailed review history out of its CloudKit sync. After restoring through Taumaha’s CloudKit sync, complete a new Health Check.

Older versions could have stored those details in the Taumaha CloudKit backup. Use the in-app deletion option described below to remove that backup. You can restrict Taumaha’s iCloud access in device Settings. Apple Privacy Policy

Apple’s whole-device iCloud Backup is separate and can include app data, depending on your device backup settings. What iCloud Backup includes

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Taumaha can write completed workouts, effort, cardio distance, recovery sessions and associated samples. With energy enabled, Taumaha reads recent body weight and active energy from Apple Health on the iPhone to estimate or record workout energy. These reads are used for Apple Health integration and are excluded from Anthropic requests. You can turn off sync in Profile or change permissions in the Health app. Turning off sync leaves previously written samples in Apple Health.

Optional Anthropic sharing

Taumaha asks you to allow sharing before it contacts Anthropic. The app uses your own Anthropic API key, stored in this iPhone’s Keychain, to send requests directly to Anthropic. Taumaha’s developer does not receive the key or requests.

Next-session reviews may send exercise names, set measurements and notes, routine targets and rule-based proposals, including up to three prior sessions for the relevant exercises. The live coach can send your workout title and plan, recent sets and targets, records, whether a note indicated pain, your question and earlier coach comments. Health Check answers and data read from Apple Health are excluded. Avoid including sensitive information in free-text notes or questions you choose to share.

Anthropic processes requests under your Anthropic account’s terms and retention settings. Turning off sharing or removing the key in Profile stops future Taumaha requests. To manage data Anthropic already received, use Anthropic’s privacy and support channels. Taumaha keeps coach messages on the iPhone and prunes messages older than 90 days when it loads or saves them. These messages are excluded from iCloud sync. Anthropic API retention

Access, correction and deletion

Use the app to view and correct your profile, Health Check, workouts and recovery sessions. Profile offers CSV export for completed workouts and routines. Other app data can be reviewed in the relevant screens.

Under Profile → Your Data → Delete all data, choose deletion from this iPhone alone or from this iPhone and the iCloud backup when available. If you keep the backup, iCloud can restore its contents. The combined option deletes the backup first and keeps local data if backup deletion fails. Copies exported to another app or location are managed there.

Use Profile → Apple Health → Delete Taumaha data from Apple Health to remove Taumaha-created samples and turn off sync. Apple Health must allow deletion. If permissions prevent removal, restore the necessary access and retry, or remove the samples in the Health app.

Local training and recovery records are kept until you remove them or reset the app. Apple may provide diagnostic reports to the developer if you choose to share diagnostics. If you email support, the developer receives the information you include and uses it to respond.
